The granny knot is two trefoils of the **same** handedness tied one after the other on a single loop (a connected sum, written 3₁ # 3₁).

It looks almost exactly like the square knot (#8), which uses two trefoils of opposite handedness. The two have the same Alexander polynomial and the same six crossings, so they are easy to confuse. The Jones polynomial separates them: the granny knot is chiral, while the square knot is its own mirror image.

Sailors know the difference too. A granny knot slips under load far more easily than a square knot.
