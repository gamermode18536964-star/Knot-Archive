The figure-eight knot is the only knot with four crossings.

Unlike the trefoil, it is **amphichiral**: it can be deformed into its own mirror image. You can see a hint of this in its Jones polynomial, which reads the same forwards and backwards.

It is the simplest hyperbolic knot. The space around it has a hyperbolic geometry with volume about 2.0299, the smallest volume of any hyperbolic knot complement.
