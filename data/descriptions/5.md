5₂ is the three-twist knot: two strands twisted around each other three half-turns, then hooked together with a clasp. It belongs to the family of twist knots, together with the trefoil and the figure-eight knot.

It is chiral, so it cannot be deformed into its own mirror image. The two versions have mirrored Jones polynomials but the same Alexander polynomial. Like every twist knot it is a two-bridge knot, and it is hyperbolic.

In this archive it first appeared at index 8. Its first braid had six crossings; untangling the diagram brought it down to five.
