The unknot is a closed loop with no knotting at all. Every diagram of it, however tangled it looks, can be smoothed out into a plain circle.

It is the most common result in this archive's search: many braids close up into a loop that simply falls open. In the first 15,000 indexes it turned up 5,053 times.

Recognising the unknot is a famous problem. It is known to be decidable, and a proof that a diagram is unknotted can be checked efficiently (the problem is in NP). Whether some knotted loop could have Jones polynomial equal to 1, the same as the unknot, is still an open question.
