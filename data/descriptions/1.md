The trefoil is the simplest knot that can't be untied: three crossings, and no way to draw it with fewer.

It is **chiral**. The left-handed and right-handed trefoils are mirror images that can never be deformed into each other, a fact first proved by Max Dehn in 1914. Their Jones polynomials are mirrored versions of each other, which is how this archive tells them apart, although it lists both under one entry.

The trefoil is also the (2, 3) torus knot: it can be drawn on the surface of a doughnut, winding twice one way and three times the other.
