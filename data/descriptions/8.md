The square knot, or reef knot, is two trefoils of **opposite** handedness tied one after the other on a single loop (a connected sum, written 3₁ # 3₁).

Because its two halves mirror each other, the whole knot is amphichiral: it can be deformed into its own mirror image. That is exactly what separates it from the granny knot (#6), which shares its Alexander polynomial and crossing number.
